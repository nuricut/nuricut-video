Frequently asked questions

Is it really completely free?

Yes. There's no sign-up, no payment and no usage limit, and we never add a watermark. Because your files are processed on your own device instead of our servers, running the site costs almost nothing — so we can keep it free.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

No. All compression happens inside your browser, and your files never leave your device.

How does "Target file size" work?

We calculate the quality that fits your target based on the video's length, then compress. If the result is slightly too big, it automatically retries. Longer videos get lower quality, so trimming to just the part you need gives a sharper result.

Why is compression slow?

Your own computer does the work, so speed depends on the video length and your device. Lowering the resolution or trimming the clip makes it much faster.

What's the maximum file size?

Up to about 2.5GB. Large files work because we read only the parts we need instead of loading the whole file into memory, but long videos can take a while. Lower the resolution or trim the clip to speed things up.
