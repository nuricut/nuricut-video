Frequently asked questions

Is it really completely free?

Yes. There's no sign-up, no payment and no usage limit, and we never add a watermark. Because your files are processed on your own device instead of our servers, running the site costs almost nothing — so we can keep it free.

Which fill mode should I choose?

If the person or subject is in the middle of the frame, "Fill" usually looks best. If everything in the frame needs to stay visible, use "Blurred".

Does it work for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok?

Yes. All three recommend a vertical 9:16 format — just upload the converted MP4 file.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion happens only inside your browser, and your files never leave your device.
