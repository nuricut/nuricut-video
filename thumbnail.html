Frequently asked questions

Is it really completely free?

Yes. There's no sign-up, no payment and no usage limit, and we never add a watermark. Because your files are processed on your own device instead of our servers, running the site costs almost nothing — so we can keep it free.

How are the smart picks chosen?

We analyze frames from across your video and filter out blurry or shaky shots, frames that are too dark or too bright, and blank screens — then pick the sharpest, most colorful moments. Press again to get a new set that doesn't repeat what you've already seen.

What size should a YouTube thumbnail be?

YouTube recommends 1280×720 (16:9). Choose the "1280×720" size and videos with a different shape will be cropped from the center to fit.

My video won't play.

Some formats, like AVI and certain MKV files, can't be played directly by browsers. Convert it to MP4 with our video compressor first, then try again.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

No. Captures happen only inside your browser, and your files never leave your device.
