How to use nuricut.video

Every tool works the same way. It takes less than a minute to learn.

  1. 1Choose a video
  2. 2Pick your options
  3. 3Press start
  4. 4Download the result
🗜️Compress video📱Make a vertical Short🎞️Make a GIF🖼️Extract thumbnails

🗜️ Compress video

Go to tool →
  1. Drop your video onto the box, or click it to choose a file.
  2. (Optional) Use Trim to keep only the part you need — pause the video and press Use current time to set the start and end.
  3. Choose how to compress: By quality (Light / Medium / Strong) or Target file size (10MB, 25MB, 50MB, 100MB or a custom size).
  4. Leave the resolution on Auto unless you need something specific. Tick Remove audio for an even smaller file.
  5. Press Compress video, check the result and press Download.

💡 Tip Sending to Discord? Pick 10MB. Attaching to an email? Pick 25MB.

📱 Make a vertical Short

Go to tool →
  1. Choose a horizontal (landscape) video.
  2. Pick how to fill the 9:16 screen while watching the preview: Blurred (shows everything), Fill (crops the sides — choose Left / Center / Right) or Black bars.
  3. Choose the resolution: 720×1280 is faster, 1080×1920 is sharper.
  4. Trim the video to 3 minutes or less if needed.
  5. Press Convert to vertical video, download it and upload it to YouTube Shorts, Reels or TikTok.

💡 Tip If the person is in the middle of the frame, Fill usually looks best.

🎞️ Make a GIF

Go to tool →
  1. Choose a video. The first 5 seconds are selected automatically.
  2. Adjust the start and end of the clip. Under 15 seconds is recommended.
  3. Choose the width (480px recommended) and smoothness (15 recommended).
  4. Press Make GIF and download the result.

💡 Tip To make the GIF smaller, use a shorter clip, a smaller width and smoothness 10.

🖼️ Extract thumbnails

Go to tool →
  1. Choose a video and pick the image size (e.g. 1280×720 for YouTube) and format (JPG or PNG).
  2. Pause on the moment you like and press Capture this frame — or press 8 smart picks to let us find sharp, good-looking frames automatically.
  3. Press the button again to get new picks that don't repeat the previous ones.
  4. Press Save under any image you want to keep.

💡 Tip Smart picks skip blurry, dark and blank frames for you.

Troubleshooting

My video won't open or preview.

Some formats like AVI or certain MKV files can't be previewed by browsers. You can still compress them — the result is a standard MP4 that works everywhere.

It seems stuck or very slow.

Your own device does the work, so long or high-resolution videos take time. Keep the tab open and in front (minimized tabs run slower). Lowering the resolution or trimming makes it much faster.

Processing failed.

Try a lower resolution or a shorter clip, close other heavy tabs, and use the latest Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, but a computer is much faster for long videos.

Are my files safe?

Yes. Files are never uploaded — everything happens inside your browser and disappears when you close the page.

Supported formats

  • Video: MP4, MOV, WebM, AVI, MKV
  • File size: up to about 2.5GB
  • Browsers: latest Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari
