FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no payment, no limits, and no watermark on your files.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything happens inside your browser on this device, and files are never sent to or stored on a server. Remembering your file while you switch tools also happens only inside this browser, and "Clear" removes it.

Can it open password-protected PDFs?

Not yet. Password removal may be added later.

Can I chain tools together?

Yes. On the result screen press "Continue with this result in another tool", then pick another tool from the tabs to keep working on the PDF you just made.
